Connect Accounts

Turns your Meta login into the three values the bot needs. One time only.
Everything here happens inside this browser tab. Your app secret and token are sent only to Meta's own API (graph.facebook.com) and are never stored, logged, or sent anywhere else. Close the tab and nothing remains.

Step 1 — paste your App Secret

Get it from your app's Basic Settings → App secret → Show → copy.

Step 2 — your secrets

Add all three to both repos: memo-social-bot and memo-social-brain → Settings → Secrets and variables → Actions → New repository secret.
