## 🔧 A §5 story-disclaimer defect, found and partly fixed

**The playbook's instruction does not work as written.** §5 says to put the legal/tax/lending
disclaimer in a story's `explain` line. **On a `quiz` story with `reveal: false`, `explain` never
renders.** `bot/render.py:1144` gates it:

```python
reveal = ans is not None and (c.video or (c.story and d.get("reveal")))
...
if reveal:
    if d.get("explain"):
        y, t = _panel(c, d["explain"], ...)
```

A reel (`c.video`) reveals and renders it. A story with `reveal: false` does not. So the disclaimer
is in the file, passes every sweep that greps the file, and **is not on the graphic.**

Two live instances, both lending-adjacent `quiz` stories with `reveal: false` and the disclaimer
only in `explain`:

- **`queue/2026-11-17/s1`** (rate vs APR) — **already posted** Nov 17 07:25 PT to Instagram and
  Facebook. Went out bare. Too late.
- **`queue/2026-11-18/s1`** (which escrow mistake kills a loan) — scheduled 07:02 PT Nov 18, not yet
  posted. **Fixed this run:** the disclaimer now also sits in `subhead`, which renders on every
  template. The two stale `s1` jpgs were deleted so `prepare.yml` re-renders them — `bot.render`
  skips an item whose media already exists unless `--force` is passed (`render.py:1567`), so editing
  the YAML alone would have left a stale graphic and a file that falsely claimed to be compliant.

**This is the same defect shape as the Nov 6 Prop 19 story** the playbook flagged, and the Nov 18
note recorded the sweep as clean on the explicit grounds that the `explain` line "renders." It does
not. **Mentor should rewrite §5 to say `subhead`, not `explain`**, and the nightly sweep should check
the rendered field rather than the file. Nov 19's own `s1` and `s2` both carry their qualifier in
`subhead`.
