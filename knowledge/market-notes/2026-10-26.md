## New this run — LA County property tax, 1st installment

The seasonal item from playbook §4 (Nov 1 / Dec 10). Verified on **two official LA County pages**
this evening, which agree on the dates:

Source A: LA County Treasurer and Tax Collector, "Secured Property Taxes General Information"
<https://ttc.lacounty.gov/secured-property-taxes-general-information/>
- Verbatim: "Annual Secured Property Tax Bills are prepared and mailed by the Treasurer and Tax
  Collector in October of each year."
- Verbatim: "The first installment of your tax bill is due on November 1 and becomes delinquent by
  the close of business on December 10."
- Verbatim: "The second installment of your tax bill is due on February 1 and becomes delinquent by
  the close of business on April 10."
- Verbatim: "Each installment is subject to penalties if not received by the Treasurer and Tax
  Collector by the aforementioned dates, and the second installment is also subject to costs in
  addition to penalties if not paid timely." **This page states no percentage.**

Source B: LA County Property Tax Portal, "Annual Secured Property Tax Bill"
<https://www.propertytax.lacounty.gov/Home/AnnualSecuredProperty>
- Verbatim: "The Annual Secured Property Tax Bill is mailed on or before November 1 of each year."
- Verbatim: "The first installment payment is due November 1. It becomes delinquent and will be
  subject to a **10 percent penalty** if payment is not received or postmarked by the United States
  Postal Service (USPS) on or before **December 10**."
- Verbatim: "The second installment payment is due February 1. It becomes delinquent and will be
  subject to a **10 percent penalty and a $10 cost** if payment is not received or USPS postmarked
  on or before **April 10**."
- Also on the page: $50.00 returned check charge. Not an installment penalty — don't present it as one.

Notes and limits:
- The two pages word the mailing date differently ("in October" vs "on or before November 1").
  Content says the bill goes out in October and is due Nov 1 — true on both readings.
- **Only Source B states the 10% figure.** Cite Source B whenever the 10% appears.
- **Neither page states** a weekend/holiday rollover rule or the July 1–June 30 tax year.
  Dec 10, 2026 is a Thursday, so the question does not arise this cycle. Do not assert a
  rollover rule in content.
- These are LA County dates. Orange, Riverside and San Bernardino counties run the same statutory
  calendar, but their pages were not read this run — content says "LA County" and tells people in
  other counties to check their own collector.

## New this run — California documentary transfer tax (for the seller net sheet piece)

Source: LA County Registrar-Recorder/County Clerk, "Documentary Transfer Tax"
<https://www.lavote.gov/home/recorder/property-document-recording/documentary-transfer-taxes/general-info>
- Authority: "Per Revenue & Taxation Code Sections 11911-11933."
- County rate, verbatim: "at the rate of fifty-five cents ($0.55) for each five hundred dollars
  ($500), or fractional part thereof." That is **$1.10 per $1,000**.
- Computed on consideration or value "exclusive of the value of any lien or encumbrance remaining
  thereon at the time of sale."
- **Some cities charge their own tax on top**, listed on the same page:
  - City of Los Angeles (eff. 7/1/26): **$2.25 per $500**, plus 4% of total value on sales above
    $5,400,000 and 5.5% at or above $10,900,000.
  - Culver City (eff. per page): 0.45% at $1,499,999 or less, rising to 1.5% / 3% / 4% in bands.
  - Santa Monica: $3.00 per $1,000 up to $4,999,999, then $6.00, then $56.00 per $1,000 at $8M+.
  - Pomona: $2.20 per $1,000. Redondo Beach: $2.20 per $1,000.
- **The page does NOT say who customarily pays it.** Do not claim the seller always pays — say it
  is customarily negotiated and appears on the settlement statement.
- Worked county-only figures (my arithmetic off the stated rate, rounded up per the page's method):
  a $900,000 sale = **$990**; an $855,000 sale = **$940.50**.
