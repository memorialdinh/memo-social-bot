The week plan's instruction on this row is unambiguous and was followed: **"Cite FHFA's own release
only… A lender's pre-announced limit is not the conforming limit; do not state a 2027 figure without
FHFA."** A trade-press number is not FHFA's release. The playbook §6 newswire workaround does not
rescue it either — that rule covers an issuer whose own release is carried verbatim by a newswire
(C.A.R. → `prnewswire.com`, Freddie Mac → `globenewswire.com`), and FHFA does not distribute this
announcement that way. So the planned no-fetch `stat` kept the `p1` slot and the announcement was
left unwritten rather than written from journalism.

**What Memo or Mentor needs to do:** approve a fetch of `fhfa.gov` (or paste FHFA's release text
into the repo) and this becomes the strongest `p1` of the quarter — the 2027 baseline, the high-cost
ceiling, and what the LA/Orange County limit means for a conforming offer. Two things make it worth
the trouble: the limit is the one number that changes what a buyer can borrow without a jumbo loan,
and the *explainer* on conforming limits is already burned, so **the news is the number**. It is also
perishable — by the second week of December every lender's email will have carried it.
