Assessments"** (<https://www.dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/refbook/ref16.pdf>), says the opposite of the
planned hook. Verbatim: **"The amount of any unpaid assessment(s) will not appear on the property
tax bill, but will be separately levied and collected."**

BOE's Mello-Roos FAQ and both county tax-collector pages — the sources that would have settled
whether and when a Mello-Roos special tax does appear on the annual secured bill — were blocked.
**So no claim about where the charge appears was made anywhere in tomorrow's content.** The angle
was rebuilt on what the DRE text does state cleanly, and the `term` was changed from "Mello-Roos"
to **"Special assessment"**, since the DRE text does not use the phrase "special tax" or name
community facilities districts at all.

Quotes used, verbatim from that document:
- "Special assessments are levied for the cost of a public improvements or services such as
  streets, sewers, irrigation, and drainage." *(the grammatical error is in the original)*
- **"These assessments are not based on the value of the property."** ← the whole teaching point
- "Civil Code Section 1102.6b requires that a seller of one to four dwelling units disclose a
  Mello-Roos assessment."
- Prop 13, p. 430: "In June of 1978, California voters approved Proposition 13, amending the State
  Constitution so that the maximum annual tax on real property is limited to one percent of 'full
  cash value' (market value) plus a maximum of two percent annual inflation factor based on the
  Consumer Price Index (CPI)..."

**Limits on this source, recorded:** the fetched PDF carries **no printed edition or revision
date**, and its internal dates (a 2007 income limit, a 2009 unified credit table, a 2010 FICA wage
base) indicate an **older edition**. It is used only for (a) the definition of a special assessment,
(b) the not-based-on-value statement, (c) the §1102.6b disclosure citation, and (d) Prop 13's 1% /
2% limits — all statements of law rather than market figures, which is why the missing edition date
does not put a dateless number on a graphic. **Nothing about duration, the levy formula, CFDs, or
the Notice of Special Tax appears in tomorrow's content**, because no source for any of it loaded.
Also not used: the DRE line that Mello-Roos is "not appropriate for inclusion in the land value of
the parcels" — accurate to the document but an assessment-practice point, not consumer-useful.
