## Prop 19 (p1 / s1) — what was used and what was deliberately left out

Facts used, all from the two BOE sources above:
- Parent-child rules **operative February 16, 2021** (the proposition itself effective Dec 16, 2020).
- The home must have been the **transferor's principal residence** and must **become the
  transferee's** principal residence; **a rental does not qualify**; at least one eligible transferee
  must live there.
- **Homeowners' or disabled veterans' exemption within one year** of the transfer; **form BOE-19-P
  within three years** or before transfer to a third party, whichever is earlier.
- **Value limit = factored base year value + $1,044,586** for transfers Feb 16, 2025 – Feb 15, 2027;
  value above the limit is **added to the base year value**.

**Left out on purpose:**
- **Family farms.** The BOE page covers them under the same form and deadline, but the week plan
  scoped this piece to a family home's tax base and a second asset class would not fit four steps.
- **Probate and trust mechanics, and anything about how the transfer itself is accomplished.**
  "Inherited/probate homes" is a burned topic in CONTENT_BRIEF; this piece is the **property tax base
  only**, which is how the week plan scoped it, and that is what kept it fresh.
- **The grandparent-grandchild rules**, the pre-2021 Prop 58 comparison, and the "moved out later"
  reassessment mechanics — all on the page, none needed, each would have added a figure or a date to
  defend.
- **The next adjustment date (February 2027).** It is sourced and true, but stating a future figure
  change on a graphic invites it being read as a prediction. It is recorded here instead.
- No dollar figure appears anywhere except the $1,044,586 value limit, which carries its period on
  the graphic (`steps` step 4) and in all four captions.

All four captions say **general information, not legal or tax advice**, and send the reader to their
**county assessor and their own attorney or CPA** rather than resolving anyone's situation.
