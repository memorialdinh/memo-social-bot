**CalHFA MyHome — the day's one fetch, and it loaded.** This is the first time this bot has been
able to run an assistance-program topic (playbook §3 names it as what earns first-time buyers).
Verified from CalHFA's own pages, read Nov 22, 2026. The program bulletin is the authoritative one
of the three and is the only one carrying a revision date: **"Last Revised: October 19, 2026."**

Mechanics confirmed (all CalHFA wording, no figures taken beyond the caps below):
- MyHome is a **deferred-payment, simple-interest subordinate loan that may only be used with a
  CalHFA first mortgage**, and **must be in 2nd lien position** when layered with other programs.
  *(bulletin, rev. Oct 19 2026)*
- **"CalHFA does not lend money directly to consumers."** It works through approved lenders, who
  qualify the borrower and make the loan; the program is **"only available through a CalHFA-approved
  lender."** *(myhome.htm + bulletin)*
- **Homebuyer education is required.** One occupying first-time borrower per transaction must
  complete counseling and **obtain a certificate of completion** through an eligible organization.
  *(myhome.htm)*
- **Occupancy: all borrowers must occupy as their primary residence within 60 days of closing.**
  Non-occupant co-borrowers and co-signors are not allowed. *(bulletin)*
- **Repayment is deferred for the life of the first loan**, then due at the earliest of: transfer of
  title, sale, payoff of the first loan, refinance of the first loan, or a recorded Notice of
  Default. *(bulletin — the two HTML pages do not list the triggers)*
- **Income limits are set by the county the property is in**; no dollar figures are printed on any
  of the three pages. No sales-price limit is stated on any of them. *(bulletin)*
- Assistance is capped at **3.50% (FHA / CalPLUS FHA) or 3.00% (conventional, USDA, VA) of the
  lesser of sales price or appraised value**; max CLTV 105%. First-time buyer = no ownership
  interest in a principal residence in the previous three years. *(bulletin, rev. Oct 19 2026)*
- Sources: <https://www.calhfa.ca.gov/homeownership/programs/myhome.pdf> (bulletin, rev. Oct 19
  2026), <https://cloud.calhfa.ca.gov/homebuyer/programs/myhome.htm>,
  <https://www.calhfa.ca.gov/homebuyer/programs/>

**No numbered "Steps to Homeownership" list exists on any CalHFA page.** The `process` reel's
