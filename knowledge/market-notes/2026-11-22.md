### What the pages actually say, and what was used

- PMI attaches on a conventional loan with a **"down payment of less than 20 percent of the
  purchase price"**.
- **Borrower-requested cancellation at 80%:** the borrower may request cancellation on the date the
  principal balance **"is scheduled to fall to 80 percent of the original value"**, and may ask
  earlier if extra payments have brought the balance there. The servicer is **"legally required to
  grant your request"** when the request is in writing, the borrower has a good payment history and
  is current, can certify there are no junior liens, and can show the value hasn't declined below
  original value.
- **Automatic termination at 78%:** **"your servicer must automatically terminate PMI on the date
  when your principal balance is scheduled to reach 78 percent."** The basis is **scheduled**
  amortization, not actual payments — the piece says "scheduled" on the graphic for that reason.
- **Midpoint backstop:** **"Your lender or servicer must end the PMI the month after you reach the
  midpoint of your loan's amortization schedule"**, and **"for 30-year loans, the midpoint is after
  15 years have passed."** Caption only; the graphic carries the two numbers that matter.
- **"Original value"** generally means the contract sales price or the appraised value at purchase,
  whichever is lower.
- **Scope, and it is in the caption because it is material:** the rules describe **"mortgages for
  single-family principal residences"** that **"closed on or after July 29, 1999."** FHA and VA
  **"have different requirements"**, and **"if your lender is paying for your mortgage insurance,
  different rules apply."**
